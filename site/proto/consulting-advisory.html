The grounded leadership reality

Grip-Tightener or Fire-Extinguisher?

When the pressure spikes, most leaders react in one of two ways: they become the Grip-Tightener (trying to micromanage every detail) or the Fire-Extinguisher (running in ten directions at once). Neither is a character flaw. It is a sign that anxiety and burnout have overwhelmed the nervous system. Powered by Positive Intelligence (PQ) and The 3R System™, we rewire the mental habits that drive second-guessing and team friction.
